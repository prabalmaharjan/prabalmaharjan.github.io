I'm a PhD student studying Programmed DNA Elimination, with a focus on the
mechanism of DNA breakage in *Ascaris* and identifying the proteins associated
with this process.

**Research techniques I use:**
- Whole genome sequencing
- DRIP-seq
- END-seq
- Ultracentrifugation
- DNA pulldown assays
- Antibody development
- Protein expression and purification

Feel free to explore my articles for updates on my research.
